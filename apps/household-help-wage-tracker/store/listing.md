## App title
Hisaab — Staff Wage Tracker

## Short description (max 80 chars)
Track domestic staff attendance and wages. No account, no ads, works offline.

## Full description

Keeping track of your maid, cook, or driver's attendance and wages in a notebook or from memory
often leads to disagreements at month-end about how many days were worked and how much is owed.
Hisaab keeps a simple, always-up-to-date record on your phone.

What Hisaab does:
• Add each staff member with their monthly or daily wage
• Mark each day as present, absent, half-day, or paid leave
• Record advances as you give them
• See exactly how much you owe, updated continuously — not just at month-end
• Settle the month with one tap, and start fresh

Why Hisaab is different:
• No account, no sign-up, ever. Hisaab does not request internet access at all — your staff's
  attendance and wage data never leaves your phone, and this isn't just a promise: the app
  literally has no permission to connect to the internet.
• Available in English and Hindi.
• Back up your data to a file you control, whenever you choose — no forced cloud sync.
• No ads. No tracking. No unnecessary permissions.

Hisaab is a small, focused app built to do one thing well: settle what you owe your household
staff, accurately and without disputes.

## Category recommendation
Finance (secondary: Productivity) — matches how the closest competitors found in research
(ServiceBook, MaidExpense) are categorized, and fits the "track money owed" core function better
than a generic "Tools" categorization.
