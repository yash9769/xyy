Hisaab does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to us or to any third party.

## What Hisaab stores
Hisaab stores, only on your own device, the information you choose to enter: names of staff
members you employ, their wage rate, daily attendance records, advance payments, and settlement
history. This data is never transmitted anywhere — Hisaab does not request internet access, and
has no capability to send data over a network.

## What Hisaab does not do
- No account or sign-up of any kind.
- No advertising, and no advertising SDK is included.
- No analytics or usage tracking of any kind.
- No data is shared with, sold to, or transmitted to any third party, because no data leaves
  your device.

## Backup
If you choose to export a backup, Hisaab creates a file on your device that you then choose where
to send (via Android's own share sheet) — this is your decision each time, not an automatic
upload.

## Changes to this policy
If a future version of Hisaab adds any capability that changes what is described above, this
policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described in that
version's release notes.
